The Print Shop paper & canvas, made to order

Every piece in the gallery can be printed and hung on a wall. Nothing sits in a warehouse — each one is made when you ask for it.

The Formats

Two Ways To Hang It

I

Fine Art Paper

Heavyweight archival matte stock. Deep blacks, no glare, and the texture the originals were painted for. Ships flat or rolled depending on size — ready to frame however you like.

  • Portrait  — 12×16  ·  18×24  ·  24×32
  • Landscape — 16×12  ·  24×18  ·  32×24
  • Custom sizes on request
II

Gallery Canvas

Stretched over a wooden frame with wrapped edges, arriving ready to hang straight out of the box. The heavier weave suits the big atmospheric pieces — the drakes, the cityscapes, the wide ones.

  • Portrait  — 12×16  ·  18×24  ·  24×32
  • Landscape — 16×12  ·  24×18  ·  32×24
  • Custom sizes on request
Pricing depends on format, size and where it's going, so it's quoted per order rather than listed here. Send a note with the piece and size you have in mind and you'll get a number back — no obligation.

How It Works

Three Steps

I

Pick The Piece

Find it in the catalog below and hit request this print. That opens an email with the title already filled in — just add the format and size.

II

Get A Quote

You'll get a reply with pricing, turnaround and shipping for exactly what you asked for. Ask about framing, odd sizes or a piece that isn't listed — the answer is usually yes.

III

It Gets Made

Once it's settled, the print is produced and shipped to you. Every print is made to order, so nothing is mass-run and nothing sits in inventory.
